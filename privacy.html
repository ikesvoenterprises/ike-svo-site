Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ike Svo Performance, a brand of Ike Svo Enterprises, handles information associated with this website, its digital products, and Ike Svo Coach.

Information you provide

When you contact us, we may receive your name, email address, and the content of your message. When you purchase a product, Payhip and its payment providers collect the information necessary to process the order, deliver the product, and issue software access.

Ike Svo Coach information

When you activate or use Ike Svo Coach, the app processes your license key and the fitness information you choose to enter, such as schedule, targets, workouts, check-ins, measurements, or progress photos. Your training and check-in progress is stored locally in the browser on the device you use; it is not automatically synced to an Ike Svo Performance account or cloud progress database. Use the in-app backup feature before clearing browser data or changing devices. Do not enter payment-card data, government identification numbers, passwords, or sensitive medical records into the app.

Payment information

Payments are processed by third-party commerce and payment providers, including Payhip and Stripe. We do not receive or store your full payment-card number on this website.

Automatically collected information

Our hosting, app, and commerce providers may collect basic technical information such as IP address, device type, browser information, referral source, timestamps, and site, app, or checkout activity for security, operations, fraud prevention, and analytics.

How information is used

  • To process purchases, issue licenses, and deliver digital products.
  • To provide app functionality and maintain customer access.
  • To respond to questions and provide order or activation support.
  • To protect the website, app, customers, and payment systems from misuse or fraud.
  • To comply with legal, tax, accounting, and platform obligations.

Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers that host the site or app, process checkout, deliver products, validate access, provide email services, or support legal and accounting obligations. We do not sell customers' personal information.

Retention and choices

Order, access, and support information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for customer service, recordkeeping, fraud prevention, security, and legal compliance. You may request access, correction, or deletion where applicable by contacting us.
