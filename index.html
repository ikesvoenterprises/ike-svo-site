No. It is a one-time purchase. There is no recurring monthly charge.

After checkout, Payhip provides your digital files, activation instructions, and unique license key. Follow the start guide to open and activate the app.

No. It is a self-guided educational fitness system. The app personalizes your schedule and starting targets from your inputs, but it does not provide medical, dietetic, rehabilitation, or one-on-one coaching services.

The primary program is gym-based and works best with common machines, cables, barbells, dumbbells, and cardio equipment. Exercise substitutions are included.
