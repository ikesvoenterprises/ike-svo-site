Meet the founder

Built on nearly two decades of training, education, and real-world results.

Fitness has been a major part of my life for nearly two decades. What began as a personal commitment to building strength and improving my physique grew into years of studying training, nutrition, recovery, and the fitness industry as a whole.

I later pursued formal education in health and fitness and worked directly with people to help them become stronger, improve their conditioning, and build routines they could actually maintain. That experience taught me that results come from applying proven fundamentals consistently and knowing how to adjust when progress stalls.

I created Ike Svo Performance to turn that experience into clear, practical systems. Ike Svo Coach is the next step in making those systems easier to execute every day.

Questions

About Ike Svo Performance

What does Ike Svo Performance sell?

Digital fitness systems focused on resistance training, nutrition organization, and measurable progress. The lineup includes the personalized SVO Custom Physique Blueprint, the app-guided 12-Week SVO Physique Method, and the focused 8-Week Lean Muscle System.

Which current program should I choose?

Choose the Custom Physique Blueprint if you want a plan built and reviewed around your specific goal, schedule, equipment, and constraints. Choose the Complete System if you want a structured 12-week app-guided experience. Choose the Lean Muscle System if you want a simpler four-day program and mobile-friendly tracking tools at a lower price.

Does the Complete System include the app?

Yes. The $129 Complete System includes the full SVO Physique Method, activation instructions, and one Ike Svo Coach license for use on up to two personal devices.

Are the current products subscriptions?

No. All current products are one-time purchases. There is no recurring monthly charge.

Is this individualized personal coaching?

The Custom Physique Blueprint is a personalized digital product with one included revision, not unlimited daily coaching. The other systems are self-guided educational fitness products. None of the products provide medical, dietetic, rehabilitation, or other individualized clinical care.

How are products delivered?

Purchases are securely processed through Payhip. Self-guided files and access instructions appear after checkout and are also sent to the order email. For the Custom Physique Blueprint, complete and return the intake after purchase; the personalized plan is then delivered by email, with a target of 72 hours after the complete intake is confirmed.

How do I get support?

Email ikesvoenterprises@gmail.com and include the email used at checkout plus a short description of the issue.

Important: These products provide general educational fitness and nutrition information. They are not medical advice or individualized professional care. Results vary. Consult an appropriate qualified professional before beginning if you have a medical condition, injury, dietary restriction, pregnancy, or other concern.
