Refund Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Ike Svo Performance products, including the SVO Physique Method Complete System, digital guides, and Ike Svo Coach software access, are delivered digitally. Because digital files and license access cannot be physically returned, purchases are generally final once access has been provided, except where a refund is required by applicable law.

File, license, or access problems

If a purchased file is defective, corrupted, missing, or inaccessible, or if a valid license key does not activate as intended, contact us within 7 days of purchase. Include the email used for the order and a description or screenshot of the issue. We will first attempt to restore access, correct the activation problem, or provide a working replacement.

Duplicate purchases

If you accidentally purchase the same product more than once, contact us promptly with the relevant order information so the duplicate can be reviewed.

Change of mind

Refunds are not normally provided for change of mind, lack of use, failure to complete the program, loss of a license key after delivery, loss of locally stored progress when no backup was preserved, incompatibility caused by an unsupported or improperly configured device, or dissatisfaction with fitness results.

Results and eligibility

No refund is promised based on fitness outcomes. Results vary, and the product is a self-guided educational resource rather than personalized coaching.
