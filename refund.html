If a purchased file is defective, corrupted, missing, or inaccessible, contact us within 7 days of purchase. Include the email used for the order and a description or screenshot of the issue. We will first attempt to restore access or provide a working replacement.

If you accidentally purchase the same product more than once, contact us promptly with the relevant order information so the duplicate can be reviewed.

Refunds are not normally provided for change of mind, lack of use, failure to complete the program, incompatibility caused by not having appropriate PDF or spreadsheet software, or dissatisfaction with results.
